Workload identity federation (processor v2)

edit

Set processor_version: v2 and auth_type: workload_identity to use a projected Kubernetes service account token. See Workload identity federation for prerequisites and identity settings.

filebeat.inputs:
- type: azure-eventhub
  processor_version: "v2"
  auth_type: "workload_identity"
  eventhub: "insights-operational-logs"
  eventhub_namespace: "your-namespace.servicebus.windows.net"
  consumer_group: "$Default"
  storage_account: "your-storage-account"
  storage_account_container: "your-storage-container"
