Logstash 8.19.24 Release Notes

Updates to dependencies

  • Update bundled JDK to Temurin 21.0.13+9, which no longer trusts the emSign ECC Root CA - G3 certificate authority. Windows packages still bundle Temurin 21.0.12.1+1, with emSign ECC Root CA - G3 removed from its trust store #19747
  • Update Jackson to 2.21.8 #19743
  • Update Guava to 33.7.2 #19695
